## Software

<div class="tool-grid">
  <a class="tool-card" href="https://github.com/CollepardoLab/OpenCGChromatin">
    <span class="tool-card__title">OpenCGChromatin</span>
    <span class="tool-card__text">GPU-accelerated, chemically specific chromatin model implemented in OpenMM.</span>
    <span class="tool-card__meta">Russell et al. (2025) · GitHub</span>
  </a>
  <a class="tool-card" href="https://github.com/CollepardoLab/Nucleosome_Pulling">
    <span class="tool-card__title">Nucleosome Pulling</span>
    <span class="tool-card__text">Package for setting up and running single-nucleosome pulling simulations. Ships with SLURM scripts for ARCHER2 and adapts to other HPC systems.</span>
    <span class="tool-card__meta">Goldilocks zone preprint (2026) · GitHub</span>
  </a>
  <a class="tool-card" href="https://github.com/CollepardoLab/CollepardoLab_Chromatin_Model">
    <span class="tool-card__title">Collepardo Lab chromatin model</span>
    <span class="tool-card__text">The multiscale LAMMPS chromatin model, at amino-acid and base-pair resolution, that underlies most of my simulations.</span>
    <span class="tool-card__meta">Farr et al. (2021) · GitHub</span>
  </a>
</div>
